A one-sentence pitch for a ridiculous invention that fixes a tiny everyday annoyance: mornings. Instead of a buzzer that blames you for snoozing, this alarm clock apologises for waking you, brews your coffee, and takes full responsibility for the early meeting — so you can start the day completely innocent.

## The pitch

> A self-apologising alarm clock that wakes you with a sincere sorry, brews your coffee, and takes full blame for the 7 AM meeting you never wanted.

## How it works

```sh
python3 main.py
```

`main.py` (Python 3, stdlib only) writes the pitch as a single line to `/output/entry.txt`, asserts it is one line of 1–220 characters, re-reads the file to verify exactly what was written, and prints the line with its character count.
